# AW3 decision from actual AW1/AW2 results

**New directional/adaptiveR: NOT_IMPLEMENTED / NOT_JUSTIFIED by this evidence.** This is the task's permitted negative design decision, not a placeholder claiming an adaptive algorithm was tested. Full same-input matching, own-state1468/4134 loops and matched-R control are complete. CandidateD is absent; no adaptive benefit is claimed.

1. GICP is not uniformly more reliable: same-input common-mask raw XY RMSE0.610m versus NDT0.608m. Some right/normal bends improve, segment5 worsens strongly, and69/1468 matches do not meet convergence qualification. No independent correct-peak labels exist. SituationA (clear global backend advantage) is not established.
2. Matrices do encode local geometry, but objective-dependent anisotropy/coupling differ: weakest-axis cosine median0.446. Marginal lateral energy's engineering-error Spearman is only+0.049/−0.056. SituationB does not establish that any eigenvalue-to-R map is calibrated or even monotonic. Simply normalizing/inverting information or retaining Native eigenvectors with a fitted scale would manufacture an unverified noise model.
3. OriginalFitness NDT gives0.577m filtered XY, fixed-R NDT0.612m, same fixed-R GICP0.596m. Therefore backend and likelihood effects are confounded in the original A/C comparison; the matched-R control verifies a modest conditional GICP benefit but does not beat originalA. Right3 fixed-R NDT/GICP P95≈0.831/0.833m versus original0.674m. A better raw candidate alone does not repair feedback under another engineering model. This supports examining information assumptions, not arbitrary gain/P/R scaling.
4. Native/full correlation preserves meaningful conditional shape, yet its historical closed loop is worse, not calibrated. A temporal model may matter: actual GICP body-lateral innovation lag1 correlation≈0.971. Innovation includes prediction/feedback and common map geometry; it is NOT a direct estimate of independent measurement-noise autocorrelation. Fitting an AR coefficient or inflatingQ from this correlation would be unjustified.
5. Fixed local perturbations reach nearby differing solutions (GICP up to0.319m at segment5). They are evidence of local basin/association sensitivity, not labelled wrong peaks. Neither joint agreement nor high rank establishes correctness. SituationD/E and shared time/map/lever error remain possible; duplicate NDT+GICP fusion is forbidden.

Implemented interfaces keep three meanings separate: native coupled shape diagnostics; actual candidate/convergence/inliers/initialization and repeatability; declared per-observation noise assumptions. `matcher_geometry` never produces a newR; `run_loop` selects one matching backend/one observation per cloud and uses the explicit fixed engineeringR only for the runnable GICP baseline/matched control. No hard NIS wall, cross-term removal, Bias clipping, undocumented covariance floor, GNSS-tuned cut or empirical gain is added.

Concrete next qualification is independent time/lever/map-reference evidence plus joint scan/map/source noise and temporal residual modelling on held-out blocks. [X-ICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.16335v3) analyzes per-pair directional contribution and constrains weak optimization directions; it does not license transferring thresholds into an uncalibrated6D measurementR. [ICP covariance analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/1410.7632) makes rematching and noise assumptions explicit. AW cannot supply these missing assumptions from scoreH or the artificial GICP planar covariance eigenvalues. No other paper title is treated as an implemented method.

Keep the simpler NDT baseline by default, retain GICP as a repeatable alternative for further qualified tests, and retain AV as failed historical context. Do not indefinitely add model complexity or launch CUDA GICP merely because two right-bend raw observations look closer to an engineering reference.
