# GNSS / map qualification
Result: fusion NOT_QUALIFIED; independent accuracy reference NOT_QUALIFIED. No LLA was relabeled map-base position, and no GNSS R was fabricated.

Read-only source evidence: `a2rl_vehicle/vectornav/src/vectornav.cc` queries model, firmware and hardware revision at connection (around lines 557–568), but no matching startup record or device response has been recovered for these recordings. `getTimeStamp` uses host `now()` unless startup-time adjustment is enabled; that adjustment estimates a moving host/sensor offset. Neither path proves calibrated acquisition latency. `parseGpsGroup` and `parseGps2Group` preserve raw fix and uncertainty fields. The local SDK `vn/types.h::GpsFix` enumerates only 0–3; raw logs contain 5,7,8 and production converter explicitly special-cases 8. This mismatch proves incomplete local protocol evidence, not that fix 8 is valid or invalid RTK.

`vn/compositedata.h` identifies GPS/GPS2 position uncertainty in NED. The installed material does not establish this hardware/firmware field's confidence/statistical interpretation. Production `ekf_converter.cpp` copies posu through a floor into covariance diagonals; another local consumer squares it. These disagree and neither is a calibration record. Even if a device-specific ICD establishes standard deviations, variance construction and NED-to-ENU axis/sign transform must be explicit: P_ENU = A P_NED A^T, including correlations. Generic product specifications cannot qualify a particular recorded receiver. The manufacturer's [VN-300 product page](https://www.vectornav.com/products/detail/vn-300) describes a dual-GNSS/INS product, but is not evidence that these bags used that exact model/firmware.

Production LLA-to-ENU calls use configured RTK_REF_POINT. The checked-in constant establishes a conversion convention, not the acquisition datum of a PCD map. PCD VIEWPOINT/coordinates do not certify WGS84 origin, heading, ellipsoidal/orthometric height, survey calibration or the map version active during recording. R2 metadata names a map filename but stores no map SHA and no runtime registration initialization. No alignment was fit to the estimator under evaluation.

Antenna A/B measurement reference, installed antenna/IMU/base lever arms, orientation used for lever compensation and reference epoch are not proven for these records. The converter's horizontal lever correction is commented; gps2 stores a separate measurement rather than demonstrating an independent truth receiver. Shared hardware/corrections/INS may correlate GNSS1/2 and other outputs. GNSS already fused into a production estimate cannot independently validate that estimate.

Receipt-minus-Header statistics are published per raw source in results; they measure transport/recording differences, not exposure/acquisition alignment. At 50 m/s, 1 ms unknown timing means 0.05 m displacement; at 70 m/s, 10 ms means 0.7 m. These are sensitivity calculations, not measured sensor errors.

Legally usable now: receipt/freshness/fix-code/change diagnostics and raw GNSS continuity. No absolute map position, yaw, Z or statistical fusion dimension is qualified. One position fix never determines yaw. Required evidence: actual model+firmware ICD, startup/config dump tied to bag, GNSS offset registers and surveyed lever calibration, hardware/header timing validation, map survey/datum/version and statistically interpretable uncertainty. D/E remain NOT_QUALIFIED.
