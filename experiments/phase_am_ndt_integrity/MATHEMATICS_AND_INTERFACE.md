# Geometry and candidate interface

For a plane residual under map-axis perturbation about the base origin, translation δt is meters and rotation δθ is radians. In coordinates ξ=[δt,Lδθ] (both metric components), H=[nᵀ,(p×n)ᵀ/L], L=10m. Source p is expressed relative to base origin in map axes; map normals come from local PCA. HᵀWH/Σw is a dimensionless normalized geometry Gram matrix, NOT a Fisher information or covariance. Keep rotation/translation cross blocks. Changing origin or L changes eigendirections; labels without this contract are invalid.

MapSupport uses transformed Source samples and nearest Target points within a declared radius, then20-neighbor normal fits and planarity. Equal total mass per2m spatial cell reduces dense-record domination; cells are NOT proven independent physical surfaces. Outputs: original/sample counts,nearby fraction,unique target IDs count,cell count,angular sectors,planar support,normal spectrum,normalized6D spectrum,basis and rank. These are supplemental geometry diagnostics, not actual CUDA correspondence support. Backend valid_pairs remains separately available in historical records. Per-LiDAR point contributions are UNKNOWN because frozen merged samples do not preserve point lineage.

classify separates NO_CLOUD,STALE,NOT_CONVERGED,MAP_SUPPORT_MISSING,WEAK_GEOMETRY,PARTIAL_DIRECTION,AMBIGUOUS,INCONSISTENT and UNKNOWN. A single low-score full-rank result remains UNKNOWN; absence of multiple detected peaks is not uniqueness evidence. Native valid candidate metadata remains distinct from frontend reliability. The exported private diagnostics.json has284 per-event records with Source hash,geometry,modes,state,fitness,convergence. AN consumes this file and multistart_diagnostics.json; no per-event trajectories/bases are public.

cluster performs deterministic complete-link SE(3) clustering, bounds1m/.1rad, declared as resolution not probabilistic significance. It retains member indices to recover seed IDs,score,convergence and support. An unsuccessful start is not another peak; equally good separated converged basins produce AMBIGUOUS. No same-source fit is an independent observation.5 multistart batches share exact Source/Target with AL/AJ. Their recorded AJ priors are legal historical priors, not generated by the new local/global branch; no autonomous frontend claim.

Primary methods examined: [X-ICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2211.16335v2) uses correspondence contributions to assess directional localizability and constrain registration. [LF-GICP](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.19522v1) uses a pre-regularization weighted normal field and distinguishes relative anisotropy from absolute missing normal mass. [SuperLoc](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.02901v2) predicts risk from point/normal geometric constraints before optimization. We borrow the direction/support separation, not their complete algorithms, tuned thresholds, benchmark claims or covariance policies. Supplemental PCA diagnostics are an independent prototype, not reproduction of those systems or CUDA objective derivatives.
