# Z Position Update

Evaluate supported point-plane residuals and vertical-normal proxies separately from state corrections. These depend on predicted pose, map support and association filters; they do not independently validate pure cloud jitter or static-ground stability. Typical improvement does not imply improved worst case.

This is a separate de-identified public summary. The immutable original/private AA reports, raw results and figures are retained under ignored `data/phase_aa_z_validation/`. No sensor-derived numerical results are distributed here.
