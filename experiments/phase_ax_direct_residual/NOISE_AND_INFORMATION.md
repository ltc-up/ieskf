# Noise, identities and what the posterior does not prove

Point0.05m, local map0.25m/1deg and scan0.5m/1deg are explicit **uncalibrated engineering hypotheses**, not manufacturer specifications or established conservative error bounds. The scan envelope is the already declared AW baseline, not selected by AX GNSS RMSE. No scale sweep occurs. Geometric `(1e−3,1,1)`, native Gaussian density covariance and kernel score are never called physical noise.

Exact-coordinate Source/Target copies are canonicalized before GICP kNN, preserving exact-alias information in tests. Coordinate equality is only a conservative identity policy; it can discard coincident but genuinely independent returns. A different unique sampling changes neighborhoods/normals/cell assignments, so **arbitrary density invariance is not proved**. It is not repaired merely by voxel downsampling.

Within each5m cell a common displacement/rotation latent correlates target correspondence errors; normals are held fixed during its linearized likelihood. This is a shared-correspondence warp approximation, **not** a fully random plane-normal/offset model. In particular, its rotation Jacobian uses target anchors, not a calibrated joint plane fit at the Source footprint. AF's complete random-plane identities are still absent. The extra common SE3 nuisance bounds aggregate same-frame pose information; synthetic/dense/Schur tests verify that defined model, not the physical validity of its latents.

One scan gives one marginal posterior, even though factors are evaluated repeatedly. No NDT and GICP fusion of the same Source, no repeated independent scan-information injection. However map errors, extrinsic/clock bias and Deskew driven by the same IMU history can correlate the likelihood with the prior and subsequent clouds. Block independence in the conditional model remains an assumption. Scan nuisance lag1≈0.977 is evidence of persistent fitted discrepancy, not a direct estimate of independent measurement-noise correlation or a calibrated remedy.

Real A has no numerical P collapse to zero: lap minimum eigenvalue5.57e−7; three-lap minimum5.00e−7, symmetric. Model lateral prior sigma in right3/9≈0.129/0.128m is larger than the old~0.063/0.069m, yet effective same-factor unit lateral responses≈0.0626/0.0610 are below old~0.10 because scan uncertainty/common residual and geometry jointly limit information. Direct residuals alone do not solve weak correction. More covariance or more weight is not justified by those numbers.

Reported PSD/Joseph/conditional GN covariance is a numerical statement. No absolute reference, NEES/NIS pass, calibrated posterior confidence, cross-frame independence, correct-map-peak probability or certified safety follows. The candidate is not a statistical-consistency success.
