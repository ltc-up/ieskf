## Phase AC — completed frozen capture study

Goal: obtain an admissible dynamic geometry solution from the original first prediction. Executed: A/B/C state comparison, 13 predetermined starts per association variant, six existing representative runs, 14 new tests, direct active-factor attribution, 12 endpoint micro-queries and one discarded conditional covariance transaction. Findings: original nearest-then-reject selection produces a one-factor two-cycle; nearest-admissible selection fixes that exact hole but nominally produces a five-cycle through remaining hard support/plane changes. Qualifications: nominal capture FAIL; isolated endpoint/transaction numeric PASS; continuous and longer replay admission FAIL; no new continuous run or Z-vz/accuracy claim. ## Phase AD — coherent association energy, no continuous admission

Executed: seven saved AC edges, 43 detected boundary records with one-sided evidence, 181 smooth-cell derivative tests, one compact Huber mixture, nine new synthetic tests, all13 original seeds and uniform endpoint precision probes. ## Phase AE — sampling density and information qualification

Goal: determine whether the unchanged AD continuous energy measures surface geometry or sampling/support structure. Executed:13 AD endpoint/Hessian rechecks;4 fixed-plane density arms;39 SAME-seed density fits,3 separate normal-refit fits,4 spatial train-only fits and4 exact-plane controls; full15 signed Hessian decomposition;10 new tests. Findings: pseudo-duplication alters null/plane prior and moves optima; fixed-plane nominal shifts4.68/32.66/25.42cm, basin memberships change. Proper spatial exclusion passes but metrics/coverage do not certify absolute accuracy.
