# KG-S3 A numerical evidence

`audit_cuda_derivatives.py` extracts only the exact two pure-arithmetic functions from a separately supplied private CUDA source and compiles them in a temporary host harness. It does not redistribute that source or use a map, bag, GPU, or trajectory reference. Fifteen smooth fixed-association synthetic cases exercise five positive/negative pitches and three different nonzero poses/points. All 21 independent Hessian terms are finite differenced. Original maximum absolute H discrepancy is 1.89934; the isolated approved pitch-sign correction reduces the maximum discrepancy to 3.69e-5. Original and corrected Score/gradient are identical for these inputs. Raw original/repaired H and Newton directions are retained in the JSON files.

The independent GPU test builds a synthetic Gaussian grid and samples synthetic source points. Three nonzero pitches times two step sizes exercise all 21 terms, with unchanged pair counts throughout each perturbation. Corrected H Frobenius relative error is 8.60e-6 to 1.92e-5; gradient relative error is 1.39e-5 to 2.40e-5. The original Align result, Hessian and Score are exactly identical before and after calling the corrected research evaluation API. Spatial-block Score and gradient sums equal the complete Score and gradient exactly for the tested synthetic partition. This small sample has only four occupied spatial blocks, so it does not qualify the A1 sandwich estimate.

`test_score_map.py` passes fourteen numerical/transaction tests: full 15D objective gradient/Hessian including chart second derivatives, full-P coupling into velocity and bias with one covariance update, frozen prior identity and preserved four candidates, historical-checkpoint prior use plus terminal/group deduplication, and zero-information treatment of absent geometric directions. These are mathematical tests, not localization accuracy tests.

A separate zero-angle test identifies an unresolved objective/gradient inconsistency. At one Euler angle exactly zero, `h=2e-5` stays inside the original `|angle|<1e-4` snapping region. Score finite differences are exactly zero; reported roll, pitch and yaw gradients are respectively −0.29645863, +0.09108047 and +0.03818346 for the synthetic case. This remains after the approved pitch–pitch correction. It is explicitly reported to the coordinator and has not been repaired. Smooth-region passes do not establish global derivative consistency or validity across voxel/gate boundaries.

All numerical samples are synthetic. No accuracy, statistical posterior calibration, continuous-feedback stability, or 50 ms/100 Hz result follows from these tests.

A second, larger synthetic GPU cloud occupies 16 spatial blocks. All 21 derivative entries pass again (H relative Frobenius error 5.51e-6–2.71e-5), original Align remains identical before/after the research API, and block Score/g sum errors remain zero. A1 GPU block scores→sandwich→one full15D update runs end to end with six retained directions and positive fullP (minimum eigenvalue 0.0025652). This does not calibrate R or certify accuracy. The additional candidate-failure test ensures an invalid Align branch is logged and cannot discard a valid prior branch.
