# Local state and vertical behavior
The existing 15D inertial predictor and scalar wheel update were reused with unchanged frozen configuration. Recorded static TF composes base-to-IMU rotation/lever; a single historical fused pose initializes p/R, a causal wheel initializes longitudinal speed, and zero bias means/assumed full P initialize uncertainty. Those assumptions are not a real sensor/map calibration. No historical global pose is fed back after initialization.

All 225 P entries are retained, propagated and saved privately at each produced 100Hz output. Finiteness and PSD gates run on outputs; no cross terms, K rows, Z or vz were zeroed/clipped. Missing input stops rather than silently extrapolating. Complete per-source counters and aggregate state changes are in per-input JSON.

Original loop7 before its 144.86 s stop: Z span 640.226 m, final vz -8.953 m/s, model sigmaZ 4583.390 m, max wheel dZ 0.465 m. Whole loop44: Z span 70,720.196 m, final vz -158.812 m/s, model sigmaZ 770.446 m, max wheel dZ 150.939 m, max wheel dvz 1.789 m/s. In the prior 73.43 s loop44 lap window alone the local Z output spans 3,012.034 m. Backup loop8 state metrics remain separately reported, never used to hide these failures.

These are estimator behavior under conditional initialization/model assumptions, not independent vehicle height error. They decisively fail eligibility as a reliable local odometer. Bias and full covariance cannot be declared calibrated from smoothness or numerical PSD. Map/Odom isolation does not add missing vertical observability and does not remove wheel-to-Z coupling.

Global manager runs load local trace arrays read-only and only receive copied poses. Hashes before/after prove no local trace modification by the executed map/GNSS qualification path. The new delay prototype also retains full P and repropagates from snapshots, without a map-update API. This is a software isolation boundary, not evidence of optimal joint probability fusion or stable local dynamics. Local health must be an explicit input to future global publication; the existing manager's `local_continuous` field alone does not validate local physical plausibility.
